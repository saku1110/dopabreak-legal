DopaBreak Privacy Policy

Effective date / last updated: August 13, 2026

Toshiki Sakuraya (the "Operator") sets out below how information is handled in the DopaBreak application (the "App").

1. The short version: your data never leaves your device

  • The App has no server operated by the Operator. There is no mechanism in the App that transmits your data anywhere.
  • No account is required. The App does not collect your name, email address, or any other personal information.
  • The App contains no advertising SDKs, no third-party analytics SDKs, and no tracking.

2. Information stored only on your device

The following information is stored only on your device, inside the App's own storage, to provide the App's features. The Operator cannot access or view any of it.

  • Your goals and records of achieving them
  • Statistics such as how many times you tried to open an app and how many times you chose not to
  • Your answers to the setup questions (such as estimated usage time)
  • App settings (notification schedule, theme, selected target apps, and so on)

3. Screen Time data

  • The App uses Apple's Screen Time frameworks (Family Controls / Device Activity). Using them requires your explicit permission.
  • Your app selections and usage activity are processed only on your device through Apple's system. No third party, including the Operator, can access this data.
  • Screen Time data is never used for advertising, marketing, or sharing with third parties.

4. Notifications

All notifications from the App are local notifications generated on your device. The App does not use push notifications from external servers. You can turn notifications off at any time in the App's settings.

5. Purchases

Paid plans are billed through Apple's In-App Purchase. The Operator never receives your payment details such as credit card numbers. Purchase information is handled under Apple's privacy policy.

6. Information we receive when you contact us

If you email support, the Operator receives your email address and the content of your message. This information is used only to respond to you and to fix problems, and is deleted after a reasonable period once that purpose is fulfilled.

7. Sharing with third parties

The App itself transmits none of your data. Information received through support inquiries is also never shared with third parties, except where required by law.

8. Deleting your data

  • You can delete all stored data at any time from the App's settings.
  • Deleting the App from your device also deletes all of its data.

9. Minors

If you are a minor, please use the App with the consent of a parent or legal guardian.

10. Changes to this policy

This policy may be revised to reflect changes in law or in the App's features. Material changes will be announced in the App or on this page.
