Canceling your subscription

  1. Open Settings on your device.
  2. Tap your name (your account).
  3. Tap Subscriptions, choose DopaBreak, and cancel.

If you cancel at least 24 hours before the renewal date, you will not be charged again. After canceling, you keep access to paid features until the end of the period you have paid for.

Refunds

Refunds are handled under Apple's policies. Please apply through Apple's refund request page.

Deleting your data

The App has no accounts. All data is stored only on your device. You can delete all data from Settings inside the App. Deleting the App from your device also erases its data.

Frequently asked questions

  • The breathing screen doesn't appear — Check that your Shortcuts automation is set up. The setup guide inside the App lets you review the steps and run a test.
  • I purchased but Pro isn't active — Try "Restore Purchases" in the App's Settings. If that doesn't resolve it, email us at the address above.
